# Attribution — Matcha-TTS-PL-Edge-v2

The acoustic models are those of Matcha-TTS-PL-v2 (credits below), exported as separate encoder / decoder graphs (fp32 and int8), with the stochastic duration predictor in `encoder_sdp*`. The vocoders:

## Vocoder: HiFi-GAN V2 (fine-tuned per voice)

- Architecture and initial weights: HiFi-GAN V2 VCTK checkpoint, J. Kong, J. Kim, J. Bae, "HiFi-GAN: Generative Adversarial
  Networks for Efficient and High Fidelity Speech Synthesis", NeurIPS 2020 — https://github.com/jik876/hifi-gan (MIT licence,
  code and released checkpoints), obtained from the mirror https://huggingface.co/niobures/HiFi-GAN.
- The VCTK checkpoint was trained on the CSTR VCTK Corpus (University of Edinburgh, Centre for Speech Technology Research),
  licensed CC BY 4.0 — https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443.
- Fine-tuning data: first the voice's recordings in machinekind/polish-synthetic-speech-voxcpm2 paired with mels of its
  Matcha-TTS-PL model (Matcha-TTS-PL-Edge), then its recordings in the v2 dataset (credits below) paired with mels of its Matcha-TTS-PL-v2 model; the discriminators of the universal V1 checkpoint (same repo, MIT)
  were used during training and are not distributed.
- Licence compatibility: MIT and CC BY 4.0 material may be adapted and shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution.

## Code

- `frontend/text.py`: text cleaners and symbol table from Matcha-TTS, S. Mehta, R. Tu, J. Beskow, É. Székely, G. E. Henter,
  "Matcha-TTS: A fast TTS architecture with conditional flow matching", ICASSP 2024 — https://github.com/shivammehta25/Matcha-TTS
  (MIT licence), with the Polish / mixed Polish–English cleaners of this project.
- Runtime dependencies (not distributed): ONNX Runtime (MIT), NumPy (BSD), phonemizer (GPL-3.0), espeak-ng (GPL-3.0),
  wordfreq (Apache-2.0 code; its word-frequency data has its own licences, see the wordfreq project).


Every voice was fine-tuned from Matcha-TTS-PL-Pretrain (formerly Matcha-TTS-PL) on its own recordings of the same texts (dataset below, one subset per voice). Both are credited.

Note: the training data of male_voxcpm_1 (the first voice) also included one KPWr 1.3 passage whose document record has no licence field (`kpwr-r20009`, popular-science subcorpus, source file badania.net.0.163.xml); KPWr 1.3 as a corpus is CC BY-SA 4.0. It is credited here and is not in the published dataset or the later voices.

The vocoder fine-tunes also used the first dataset, [machinekind/polish-synthetic-speech-voxcpm2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/machinekind/polish-synthetic-speech-voxcpm2) (CC BY-SA 4.0, the same voices; its text credits are in that dataset's ATTRIBUTION.md).

## Training data (machinekind/polish-synthetic-speech-voxcpm2-v2, all voices)

This dataset is released under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (`LICENSE`). It contains text from the sources below, each credited per
text in `credits.jsonl` and in every `metadata.jsonl` (columns `source`, `license`, `url`, `author`, `title`,
`modified`); the voices read the same texts, so one credit covers every recording of a text (a feminine variant `<id>f` has its own). Items marked *adapted*
were changed (numbers and abbreviations written out, a cut sentence completed, first-person forms changed to match a
speaker gender, length trimmed) — the original is at the linked source.

Licence compatibility: CC BY (NKJP, ParlaMint, Wikinews) and CC BY 3.0 (YouTube videos via YODAS) material may be
adapted and shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution; CC BY-SA 2.5–4.0 material (Wikibooks, Wikivoyage, part of
KPWr) permits later versions of the licence; OpenAssistant oasst2 texts are Apache-2.0 — the licence text is in
`LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`. All audio was generated by VoxCPM2 (OpenBMB, Apache-2.0, `LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`) with
synthetic voices designed from text descriptions (not real people). Texts marked "written for this dataset" are
original (written for this dataset, partly with Claude by Anthropic) and are released under CC BY-SA 4.0 with the rest.


Per-clip records: `credits.jsonl` (10463 entries). Texts marked *adapted* were changed (numbers and abbreviations written out, a cut sentence completed, first-person forms changed to match a speaker gender, length trimmed).
